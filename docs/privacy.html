Privacy and data handling

This personal workflow accesses only the Google account data authorized by the account owner. Drive and Sheets access is used to locate the selected folder and create, read and update the order spreadsheet.

Amazon order information is retrieved through the account owner's session. Local order exports and OAuth credentials are stored on the operator's computer; OAuth credentials are also saved in the operator's Bitwarden vault. The resulting spreadsheet is stored in the selected Google Drive folder and follows that folder's sharing permissions.

The operator retains the data until it is deleted. The account owner can delete the spreadsheet and local copies, and revoke access in Google account settings. This site has no analytics or tracking scripts.
