Amazon Orders Exporter

Amazon Orders Exporter is a personal tool for reviewing Amazon order history and preparing a Google spreadsheet with annual and monthly purchase summaries.

It requests Google Drive and Sheets access to find a folder selected by the account owner and create, read and update the spreadsheet. The spreadsheet may contain order dates, item descriptions, prices and masked payment references.
